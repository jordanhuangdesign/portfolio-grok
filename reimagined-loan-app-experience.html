Reimagining the Mortgage Application

Reframing the Mortgage Application as a Conversation

Transforming a high-friction mortgage application from a long-form experience into a guided, conversational journey designed to evolve toward AI-assisted interactions.

Role

Principal Product Designer

Ownership

Product strategy, workflow planning, UX/UI design, responsive web prototype

The Challenge

Too much paperwork and zero feedback.

Mortgage applications require borrowers to provide a large amount of information before receiving much value in return. The legacy experience exposed that complexity through a traditional long-form interface, creating cognitive overload and significant abandonment.

Key Questions

Bring time‑sensitive tasks and high‑value actions forward instead of exposing the entire pipeline at once.

The Challenge

Shaking off the shadows of the past.

With competitors launching new designs for loan applications, it was clear that loanDepot was not going to take back our lead if we aimed for incremental improvements. The new application needed to be revolutionary and future-proof.

This is however, a very conservative industry, and every change required lengthy discussion. To facilitate the discussions around loan application, I created a light tool website that is aimed to surface questions/information that are tied to valued milestones - lead generation and prequalification.

Loan Origination Planner

Structure the Application

Stakeholders can add, edit, remove and reorder application categories and individual questions rather than discussing the flow abstractly. Individual steps can even move between categories.

Connect Every Question to a Purpose

Each question can be mapped to a specific Satisfied Requirement, turning “Should we ask this?” into a more useful question:

“What requirement does this answer satisfy?”

That prevents the application from accumulating questions simply because they have historically been there.

Visualize Lending Milestones

Requirements are organized into checklists, and those checks update as application steps satisfy them. Your tool also calculates where milestones have effectively been reached in the overall flow.

The Design Pivot

From lengthy forms to interactive conversation.

The MVP experience is constrained by lack of AI capabilities at the time design was introduced. The questions will be deterministic rather than AI-driven. Borrower responses trigger predefined follow-up questions, conditional fields, and skipped steps based on established application logic.

The interface allows easy AI integration with the virtual agent controlling pacing, keeping questions in context, acknowledging responses, and following up where responses are incomplete or require clarification.

This design fits within organizational constraints while successfully modernizing the UI without extensive backend upgrades. This was especially valuable for traditional businesses adapting to AI technologies.

The concept establishes a new interaction model for the mortgage application—reducing visible complexity, adapting the experience to the borrower, and creating a foundation for future AI-assisted workflows.

Just as importantly, the Loan Origination Planner created a shared framework for determining what information belongs in the application, when it should be collected, and what business or lending requirement each step satisfies.
