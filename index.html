From Legacy POS to Mobile-First Experience

Current POS has many pages with repetitive information, sometimes with slightly different terminologies or additional details. There are also features that deals with certain pieces of information but is located in its independent page. This may not be a show stopper on a larger desktop screen, but on a small mobile screen constantly needing to jump from page to page is an inconvenient and inconsistent experience.
