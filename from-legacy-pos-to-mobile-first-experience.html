Mortgage POS  •  Mobile Product Design

From legacy POS to mobile-first experience.

This concept re-architects a complex mortgage POS for mobile, prioritizing what loan officers need most while reducing redundancy and reorganizing workflows around the loan origination journey.

Role

Principal Product Designer

Ownership

Product strategy, IA, UX/UI design, prototype

The Challenge

Translating a complex desktop platform into a genuine mobile experience.

The existing POS exposed a broad set of tools and information structured around the desktop screen size. The mobile concept reframes the product around what a loan officer needs in the moment: priorities, actions, and the next logical step in the loan journey.

1

Surfacing Priorities

Bring time‑sensitive tasks and high‑value actions forward instead of exposing the entire pipeline at once.

2

Reduce Redundancy

Remove duplicated information and collapse complexity so the experience fits the mobile form factor without feeling cramped.

3

Re-Architect Info.

Organize tools and information in a logical, chronological order aligned with the loan origination process.

before & after

From full pipeline visibility to actionable focus.

Existing Desktop POS

Broad visibility, dense filtering, many steps before action.

Mobile-first Concept

Priorities first, preset filters, direct entry into action.

key design decision

Re-organizing loan information and services around the origination journey.

Information and tools are reorganized around the sequence of the origination process, reducing navigation overhead and creating a structure that surfaces any blockers or incomplete tasks that could impact origination time. Integration of services further removes complexity by presenting all necessary components in a linear flow.

Existing Desktop POS Control Panel

Redundant information is spread across multiple pages, while critical tools and services are scattered or buried several layers deep.

A Chronological Workspace -

Loan File

  • Sections of loan are organized into cards with services like credit check or income validation nested inside each sectional page.
  • Color-coded card styling shows loan officer status of each step in one glance.
  • A more intuitive workflow can reduce training overhead and help loan officers complete tasks faster.

Explore the prototype

Launch Prototype

outcome

An evolving product with overwhelming support.

The concept established a mobile-first architecture for bringing the core POS workflow to loan officers away from their desks. The next step was user validation with loan officers before production refinement.

Next Steps

While the concept moves through user testing and refinement, I was also planning the next 2–3 years of product evolution. The architecture is designed to support a gradual transition from traditional UI patterns to AI-assisted workflows, allowing specific sections of the experience to become more conversational and automated as AI capabilities become reliable enough for production use.
